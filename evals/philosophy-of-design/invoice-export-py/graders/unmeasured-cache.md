PASS if the review questions the module-level `_results` cache as unjustified complexity (nothing shows the export is slow, a bare 3600-second expiry stands in for any rule about when an entry is stale, caching is mixed into the export logic) and proposes measuring first, removing it, or giving it one owner with a clear invalidation rule. FAIL if it never mentions the cache, or only says the cache is not thread-safe or grows without bound.
